NWA Urban Centers

A tract-based map of Northwest Arkansas using combined population and workplace-job density. An urban center is a connected group of Census tracts at or above the 80th percentile. An urban core is a connected group of 95th-percentile tracts nested within an urban center.

Two-tract minimum: a single qualifying tract is intentionally not an urban center or core. At least two adjacent Census tracts must qualify together. Boundaries follow the Census tracts, not municipal borders.
